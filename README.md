
<h2 align='left'>Inspired by The Legend of Zelda</h2>

  <h3 align='left'>  Tired of the vanilla horse taming mechanics?</h2>
<h3 align='left'>Let’s try this fresh and immersive taming system!</h2>


- Now, your wild horses will be wary of players, just like in *The Legend of Zelda*, and similar to <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/flee-on-sight">*Flee On Sight*</a> .

- If you approach them too recklessly, they will get scared and bolt away. 

- Sneaking will make it much easier to close the distance.

- Approach slowly and carefully before the horse notices you.
  
- Once you're close enough, right-click with a saddle to mount and tame the horse!

#### More details will be added in future updates.
